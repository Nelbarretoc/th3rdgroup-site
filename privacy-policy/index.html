Privacy Policy

This policy explains the information used to operate the website and respond to business inquiries.

Last updated

July 18, 2026

Staging draft: this policy must be reviewed with qualified counsel and confirmed against final analytics, marketing and vendor settings before production launch.

Overview

TH3RD GROUP LLC ("TH3RD GROUP," "we," "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect and use information when you visit th3rdgroup.com or submit a business inquiry through the website.

Information we collect

Information you provide

The project inquiry form may collect your name, work email, company, website, country, service interests, project description, timeline, optional investment range, current or target markets and referral source.

Technical and source information

We may process basic device, browser, security and request information needed to deliver and protect the site. The inquiry form also records the page and campaign source associated with a submission. The current build does not install advertising pixels or behavioral analytics by default.

How we use information

  • Review and respond to project inquiries.
  • Evaluate whether TH3RD GROUP may be a relevant fit.
  • Coordinate an introductory conversation when appropriate.
  • Protect the website, prevent abuse and maintain service reliability.
  • Comply with applicable obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell personal information submitted through the project inquiry form.

Service providers and data handling

The website is prepared for hosting through Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare Turnstile is used to help prevent automated abuse. Submitted inquiries are transmitted server-to-server to HubSpot for inquiry management. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy commitments.

Information may be processed in countries other than your own. We retain inquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including business, security and legal needs.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. These rights may be subject to exceptions.

To make a privacy request, use the contact form and clearly state that your message is a privacy request. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so please do not submit confidential product formulas, credentials, financial account details or other highly sensitive information through the website form.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices, service providers or applicable requirements change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.
