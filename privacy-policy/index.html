LEGAL INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

This policy explains the information used to operate the website, understand performance and respond to business inquiries.

Last updated

September 14, 2026

We review this policy when our services, technology, vendors or legal obligations change.

1. Overview

TH3RD GROUP LLC (“TH3RD GROUP,” “we,” “us”) operates this website and provides strategy, systems, execution and market-readiness services. This policy applies to information collected through th3rdgroup.com and its first-party inquiry workflow.

2. Information we collect

Information you submit

Our project inquiry form may collect your name, work email, company, website, country, service interest, project description, timeline, investment range, current and target markets or channels, referral source and consent record.

Technical and attribution information

We may collect the first and current landing page, referring page, campaign parameters (including UTM values and gclid), page language, device category and related timestamps. Our infrastructure may also process IP address, browser data and security signals needed to deliver the site, prevent abuse and protect the form.

3. How we use information

We use information to evaluate and respond to inquiries, understand service interest, operate and secure the website, attribute business conversations to pages or campaigns, improve the customer journey, maintain records and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

4. Forms, COMPASS and service providers

Inquiry information is sent through a first-party Cloudflare function to TH3RD COMPASS, our internal CRM and operating system. COMPASS stores the submission, service classification, source attribution and consent record so authorized team members can review and manage the conversation.

We use service providers such as Cloudflare for hosting, security and delivery. If optional analytics is configured and you allow it, Google Analytics 4 or Google Tag Manager may process event and campaign data under their own terms.

5. Analytics, advertising and storage

Necessary session storage may remember the first landing page and campaign context so the same attribution can accompany an inquiry. This works without sending those details to an external analytics provider.

Optional analytics loads only after you choose “Allow analytics.” We use events such as introductory-call clicks and successful inquiry submissions to understand the path to a business conversation. We do not send form fields, names, email addresses, project descriptions or other direct personal details to analytics. Campaign parameters may reflect advertising activity; we do not claim that every advertising platform or feature is enabled.

You can change your choice using “Analytics preferences” on this page. Browser controls may also clear local or session storage.

6. Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the inquiry, business records, security and legal obligations. We use access controls, signed system-to-system requests, encrypted transport, abuse protections and operational safeguards. No method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or information about our handling of your personal information. We may need to verify your identity and retain limited records where required.

8. International processing and children

TH3RD GROUP works across markets, and providers may process information in countries different from yours. We use reasonable contractual and operational safeguards where applicable. This business website is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to hello@th3rdgroup.com. Please do not send sensitive personal information by email.
