BEFORE DEFINING THE SCOPE

Questions to prepare your next step.

Where should a business start with AI—and when is ordinary automation better?

Choose a defined task such as document interpretation, research, classification, customer-support drafts or reporting assistance. Specify grounding sources, permissions, review ownership, evaluation, cost and fallback before implementation. Routing information, reminders and deterministic checks may need no AI. Introducing an agent does not remove those controls or authorize autonomous decisions. A roadmap should prioritize a useful workflow and adoption, not promise a generic tool that can replace the business or all of its systems.
