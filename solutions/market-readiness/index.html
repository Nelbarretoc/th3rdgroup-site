HOW THE REVIEW WORKS

Source-backed research, AI-assisted organization, human review.

01

Define the target context

Confirm the product, destination market, intended channel and the decision the review needs to support.

02

Collect and structure evidence

Review supplied materials and relevant public official sources using a consistent readiness framework.

03

Organize preliminary findings

Use governed AI assistance where useful to classify information, surface gaps and maintain traceability—without replacing human judgment.

04

Review and prioritize

A human-reviewed report separates observations, open questions and recommended next actions, including where specialist input is appropriate.

REPORT STRUCTURE

02

03

04

Prioritized next steps

Actions, open questions, likely owners and areas that may require formal submission, testing or specialist review.

IMPORTANT BOUNDARY

A preliminary readiness service—not an approval.

TH3RD GROUP provides pre-validation, market-readiness, systems, growth partnership and structured advisory services. These services are not legal opinions, official approvals, government certifications, guaranteed compliance determinations or guaranteed commercial outcomes. Additional testing, documentation, formal submissions, specialist review or partner-specific requirements may apply depending on the product, market, risk profile and growth channel.
