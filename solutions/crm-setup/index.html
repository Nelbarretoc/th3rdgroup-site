COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we start.

Do we need a custom CRM?

Not necessarily. We first assess whether configuring an existing tool will meet the need. A custom system is considered when the workflow and maintenance requirements justify it.

Can the website form connect to the CRM?

Yes, subject to the selected tools, API access and agreed scope. We define validation, duplicates and what happens when delivery cannot be confirmed.

Can this support real estate or home-service teams?

We can scope inquiry tracking, assignment and follow-up for those workflows. Booking, messaging and industry-specific integrations must be confirmed for your systems; they are not assumed to be available.

Can a CRM replace spreadsheets and connect our follow-up?

A scoped CRM setup can organize customer records, sales stages, calls, email context, quotes, task owners and follow-up. For real estate or cleaning/home-service teams, start with the inquiry, assignment, appointment or estimate workflow—not a promise of a prebuilt industry product. Website intake, scheduling, WhatsApp and other messaging connections depend on available interfaces, consent and permissions. Imports, duplicate handling, reporting and maintenance are agreed before implementation.

The example demonstrates a capability, not a ready-to-use client account. Software subscriptions, data migration, outbound messaging and ongoing support are agreed separately.
