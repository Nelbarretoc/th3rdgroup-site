BEFORE DEFINING THE SCOPE

Questions to prepare your next step.

What should we prepare before opening a marketplace or online store?

Confirm the country, selling entity, product identity, channel eligibility, account access and the evidence the selected platform requests. Then define catalog, content, pricing, inventory, fulfillment, returns and ownership. Amazon Seller Central, Vendor Central, Walmart Marketplace, TikTok Shop, Mercado Libre and Shopify have different models and conditions; availability is checked for the specific market. Setup support does not guarantee account approval, retail placement or launch acceptance.

What is involved in ongoing marketplace operations?

A scoped operating plan can cover listing and catalog quality, content changes, inventory and order visibility, fulfillment/returns handoffs, promotions, reporting and issue ownership. Establish which record controls shared product and stock data across channels. Account-specific listing problems or chargebacks need their actual records and platform rules reviewed. Ongoing management and a one-time setup are different scopes; neither implies guaranteed sales or the ability to bypass a platform decision.

What should a creator or affiliate program brief define?

Identify the product, market, audience, creator criteria, language, platform eligibility, sampling plan, content usage rights, disclosures, approvals and sales attribution. TikTok Shop affiliate setup, creator recruitment, influencer partnerships and multi-country campaigns are not interchangeable deliverables. Confirm which work TH3RD and each partner would actually own before commissioning outreach or a campaign. A planning discussion is not a promise of creators, access, affiliate sales or regional representation.

How should social media and content work connect to the business?

Begin with the audience, offer, channel role and the action each piece should support. Account/page setup, a monthly calendar, posting, Instagram Reels, short-form video, photography and reporting are different deliverables. A local service company and a beauty brand need different examples and approval processes. Confirm production capacity, source assets, publishing permissions, review ownership and what is included in the agreed engagement; do not assume every content task or a sales result is included.

What should be agreed before a Google, Meta or paid-social campaign?

Define the customer, offer, country, budget owner, landing page, lead form, consent requirements, inquiry destination and conversion measurement. Google Search, Facebook/Instagram and other paid-social channels should be selected for a specific goal, not treated as identical placements. Campaign setup, creative production, management and media spend require explicit agreement and account permissions. A submitted form is not a qualified lead or a sale, and campaign results cannot be guaranteed.
