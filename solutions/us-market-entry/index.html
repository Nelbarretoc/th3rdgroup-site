Enter the U.S. with a clearer market-entry plan.

THE DECISION

A market this large rewards focus before scale.

TH3RD GROUP helps consumer brands prepare to sell in the United States by connecting the market, customer, channel, economics and operating decisions behind a viable entry plan.

We help leadership teams organize those choices into a market-entry thesis, identify the assumptions that need evidence and define the first practical route to market.

COMMON FRICTION

The opportunity is visible. The route is fragmented.

We bring the commercial and operational questions into one decision process.

01

Too many possible channels

Amazon, Walmart.com, D2C, distributors and retail opportunities compete for attention without a shared prioritization model.

02

An incomplete market case

Demand signals may be promising while positioning, unit economics, competitive context or the first customer remain unresolved.

03

Execution begins too early

Teams invest in inventory, partners or campaigns before clarifying readiness, responsibilities and the operating requirements behind the launch.

BEFORE DEFINING THE SCOPE

Questions to prepare your next step.

Should a consumer brand start with U.S. retail, Amazon or its own website?

Start with the category, intended customer, competitive evidence, commercial assumptions, pricing and operational capacity. Compare retail/distributor, marketplace and D2C paths against the same objective, then define a first channel, owners and launch gates. Korean beauty, skincare, personal care and other consumer products still require their own product context. A market-entry roadmap connects the commercial decisions; product readiness reviews and channel acceptance remain separate, scoped decisions rather than automatic approvals.
