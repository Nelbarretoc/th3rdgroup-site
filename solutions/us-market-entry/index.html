THE DECISION

A market this large rewards focus before scale.

Entering the U.S. is not one launch decision. It is a sequence of choices about the customer, geography, channel, offer, economics and operating model.

We help leadership teams organize those choices into a market-entry thesis, identify the assumptions that need evidence and define the first practical route to market.

  • 01

    A sharper market and channel thesis

  • 02

    Prioritized readiness and operating gaps

  • 03

    An executable first-stage roadmap

COMMON FRICTION

The opportunity is visible. The route is fragmented.

We bring the commercial and operational questions into one decision process.

01

Too many possible channels

Amazon, Walmart.com, D2C, distributors and retail opportunities compete for attention without a shared prioritization model.

02

An incomplete market case

Demand signals may be promising while positioning, unit economics, competitive context or the first customer remain unresolved.

03

Execution begins too early

Teams invest in inventory, partners or campaigns before clarifying readiness, responsibilities and the operating requirements behind the launch.

OUR APPROACH

Move from possibility to a decision-ready plan.

01

Frame the market case

Clarify the growth objective, priority consumer, category context, competitive signals and the assumptions that matter most.

02

Choose the entry path

Evaluate channels, geographic focus, marketplace roles and partner needs against the brand's economics and capabilities.

03

Map readiness

Organize product, documentation, content, data, inventory, workflow and team requirements before larger commitments are made.

04

Build the launch roadmap

Define stages, owners, decision gates, measures and the first coordinated workstreams required to move.
